| ADR-0051 | Separate population/comorbidity from indication: a disease named only as an enrolled population, comorbidity, eligibility criterion, or stratification factor is not an indication, and a population difference alone creates no program row. | ADR-0052 | Adopt Scope 2.0 as an additive expansion of Scope v1.1: keep the existing core-mechanism qualification path (R2) exactly as defined in ADR-0026, unchanged in breadth, and add a second, mechanism-independent obesity-purpose qualification path (R1) alongside it. An asset qualifies under R1 ∨ R2. A qualifying Program triggers asset-scoped closure for its focal asset; a qualifying Regimen qualifies only itself and never propagates qualification, or asset-scoped closure, to its component assets — a component asset must independently satisfy R1 (via its own qualifying Program) or R2. | The dataset already carried four assets held in scope only by core mechanism, with no obesity-indication row (Roche CT-868, QL Biopharm ZT003, Novo Nordisk IcoSema, Gan & Lee GZR102); removing or narrowing R2 would drop them or force a retroactive re-justification. Treating R2 as additive rather than superseded preserves early asset-scoped discovery: a newly disclosed GLP-1/GIP asset still qualifies the moment its core mechanism is confirmed, before an obesity indication is ever disclosed. Regimen-to-component propagation was evaluated and rejected: a regimen is a co-administration configuration, not a component's own development program, and letting it qualify a component would pull that component's entire unrelated pipeline into closure (for example, a partner antibody co-dosed with tirzepatide in one regimen). | Active | [Data Protocol scope](./README.md#dataset-scope), [Entities and Rows](./entities-and-rows.md#scope-class) |
